Artwork drop-in folder
======================

The launcher draws its banner from two optional files here. Nothing breaks if they are absent:
it falls back to a text title.

hero.jpg
    The background of the top banner. Exact size does not matter; it is scaled to cover the
    banner and cropped. Around 1600 x 400 works well. Face the subject toward the right third,
    because the title sits in the middle.

logo.png
    The game's logo, drawn instead of the text title. Transparent background, about 800 px
    wide. It is scaled to 66% of the banner width.

icon.ico  (optional, for Build-Launcher.ps1)
    Not used yet: the build script does not embed an icon. Add /win32icon here when there is one.

Please only put artwork here that you have the right to distribute. The game's official logo and
key art belong to Sega and Red Entertainment; a fan-made banner is the safer choice for a public
release.
