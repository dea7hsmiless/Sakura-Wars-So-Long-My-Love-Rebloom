Put your disc image here
========================

Copy your own PlayStation 2 disc image into this folder as a plain .iso:

  Sakura Wars - So Long, My Love (USA) (Disc 1) (English Voice Over).iso
  Sakura Wars - So Long, My Love (USA) (Disc 2) (Japanese Voice Over).iso

One is enough to play; both give you the voice-language switch in the launcher.

The file name only matters for telling the two apart:

  * a name containing "Disc 1" or "English" is used for English voices
  * a name containing "Disc 2" or "Japanese" is used for Japanese voices

If the name says nothing, the first .iso is used, and you can always point at a file
somewhere else in the launcher under Setup.

Do not put anything else here. No disc image is included with this download, and none
ever will be: use your own disc.
