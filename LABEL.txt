The Android APK is debug-signed and installs by sideloading. The iOS IPA is
unsigned and needs the user's own signing (Sideloadly, AltStore, or Xcode).
Both mobile builds have an on-screen pad, a "Tap to advance" switch, and use
Bluetooth controllers through SDL beside the pad. The Windows build does not
include the tap-to-advance feature. The mobile launchers use the sakura palette;
the Windows and Linux launchers default to it too, with a Classic (gold) option
under Settings -> Display.
